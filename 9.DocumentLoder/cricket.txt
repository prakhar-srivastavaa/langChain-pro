Cricket, the gentleman's game, played on a field so wide,
Where bat meets ball and the crowd cheers with pride.
The bowler runs in fast, the batsman stands tall,
A six over the boundary, the greatest shot of all.

The spinner turns the ball on a dusty afternoon,
While the wicket-keeper waits beneath the blazing sun.
Test matches last for days, a war of patience and skill,
Every run is precious, every wicket a thrill.

In the IPL stadium, the lights shine so bright,
Thousands of fans roaring into the night.
From Sachin to Kohli, legends of the game,
Cricket runs in our blood, it will never be the same.

The team walks in together, eleven men as one,
Playing for the nation until the match is won.
